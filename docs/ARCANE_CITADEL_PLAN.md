# Arcane Citadel — approved expansion direction

October 9, 2026. User approved continuing with this location after Coast.
Order: forest → swamp → city → Shipwreck Coast → Arcane Citadel.
Citadel is planned, not playable. Coast also remains in production.

This explicitly expands the previous monsters-only art scope to adult elven
humanoid opponents. Hiro remains the only playable hero. Original Adventurer
and all its assets are read-only; reuse copies/references in Expeditions.

## Roster and combat direction

| Opponent | Role | Proposed distinct moves |
|---|---|---|
| Male elven wizard | Ranged caster | Arcane volley; a breakable ward with limited duration |
| Female elven ranger | Mobile ranged fighter | Enchanted arrow; clearly telegraphed evasive shot |
| Female dark-elven dual-sword warrior | Close-range pressure | Alternating blade combination; counter stance |
| Female elven enchantress | Final boss | Binding sigil; weakening curse; empowered second phase |

Avoid unavoidable permanent control, infinite evasion and self-sustaining
healing loops. Validate numbers with the existing encounter simulations before
unlocking. These move concepts are a production proposal, not implemented skills.

Every new opponent needs idle, approach, attack, hit and down animation;
enchantress also needs an enrage/phase tell. Each needs two unique Hiro-on-enemy
paired finishers and one enemy-on-Hiro paired finisher. Preserve Hiro's identity,
body size, visible continuity and contact/release markers. Existing user-retired
finishers must stay retired. The release coverage test requires at least one
active Hiro finisher per enemy, preserving the current approved removal list;
the new-location production brief still calls for two.
