# Chapters 6–14 — local campaign completion

This pass finishes the later chapters using the registered campaign enemies and original story recordings. Chapters 1–14 are enabled locally and the later campaign is ready for player review. No website or portal package is published by this pass.

## Combat scenery

Ten dedicated 1280×760 paintings replace unsuitable combat compositions: wyvern cliffs, sewers, study, counting room, sickroom, tower, silk floor, undervault, valve room and catacombs. They provide a continuous horizontal contact surface at the fighters’ ground line instead of cliffs, water or furniture under their feet. Packed addition: **3,573,970 bytes**.

All 21 distinct late-campaign battle locations passed a browser check for three actual depth planes: distant painting, moving scenery/weather, and fixed character/contact ground. Motion pauses with the game. Screenshot lineups were inspected for foot placement and relative character size. No decoration is placed in front of Hiro.

Dialogue keeps the original paintings. Combat-specific lamp, water and cloth coordinates no longer leak into those scenes; all 12 affected dialogue locations passed a separate browser check. Morrak’s realm uses blue flame to match its original painting.

Sources: `../adventurer-expeditions-source-art/astra-v9/chapter-stages-20261014/`. Exact generation prompts/references are copied into `art/chapter-stages-20261014-prompts.json`. Rebuild with `python tools/build_story_late_stages.py`. Packed hashes and ground metadata: `../assets/expedition/story/stages/late-stages.json`. Source masters remain outside this Git repository.

## Branches, inn and endings

Two additional complete routes passed **state and combat simulation**, separately from browser coverage. The mercy route asks Nettle about the poisoned wood before negotiating, keeps the prisoners alive, uses available peaceful bypasses, recruits Amara and restores Hiwot. The ruthless route floods early, fights the bypassable opponents and ascends. Every requested route choice is asserted; unavailable choices cannot silently fall back to a different route. Both routes preserve chapter progress through actual simulated defeats/retries and retain their ending on reload.

The inn browser check passed paid healing, repeated bathhouse visits, pause, saved rest without a second charge, full-health/insufficient-point behavior and continuing the story on desktop and a 390-pixel phone viewport. All seven original ending cards passed text, narration request, mobile layout and exit-cleanup checks. This does not mean seven distinct full campaign routes were played in the browser.

## Scope

All 59 opponent identities, their Hiro/enemy paired finishers and painted projectiles were completed in the preceding art pass; they are reused here. No duplicate enemy sheets, new dialogue or new voice generation was needed. Companions remain story-only; Hiro fights alone. Optional additional Arcade encounters are a separate future addition, not inserted into the original plot in this pass.

The public site remains independently packaged: chapter one free, chapters two through five behind the existing password. Opening local chapters does not publish them or expand that package. Original Adventurer and synced shared source files are untouched.
